SUNDAR & FAMILY — HOUSE WARMING INVITATION
Netlify/GitHub Pages ready static app.

Flow:
1. Full-screen thamboolam opening.
2. Tap the existing house seal (invisible hotspot only).
3. Seal visually fades.
4. Date/time ticket rises halfway from the envelope.
5. Same ticket expands to Thirukkural 45 for 3 seconds.
6. Isaiah 32:18 for 3 seconds.
7. Blessings shrink/dock at the top.
8. Actual house photo appears large then shrinks to a centred clickable card.
9. House warming information, Calendar, Directions, Order of Events.
10. "Your presence is my present" with realistic sapling message.
11. With love, Sundar & Family.
