Animation flow
1. Traditional thamboolam opening screen.
2. Tap the house seal; only the visible seal fades, with no blue tap box.
3. The House Warming invitation rises from inside the plate/envelope.
4. Thirukkural 45 appears and remains for 3 seconds.
5. Isaiah 32:18 appears and remains for 3 seconds.
6. Both verses appear together and shrink to the top.
7. The house photograph appears below the blessings.
8. The same house component shrinks into a compact centred clickable medallion, without overlapping any text.
9. House Warming details, Google Calendar and Google Maps appear below.
10. Order of events.
11. Plant gift message.
12. With Love closing.
